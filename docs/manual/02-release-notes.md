**About the tags.** The project was developed quickly (October 30 to November 4, 2026) without release tags; the milestones above were tagged retrospectively on November 4, 2026 on the commits that completed them. Commit dates were never rewritten.

**Changes after `paper-v1`.** Everything after the paper version is a post-paper change. It does not alter the results reported in the paper, which are about `paper-v1` only.

**Before upgrading,** read the entries between your version and the new one, and [chapter 4](04-upgrading.md).
